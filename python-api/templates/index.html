This is the machine learning service of the Prefect of Discipline Management System. It helps the Prefect of Discipline act early on student behavior and review complaints faster, using the two models below.

Models

Early Intervention Violation Predictor

Logistic Regression

Estimates how likely a student is to commit the same violation again, so the Prefect of Discipline can step in before it happens.

  • Looks at how often the student committed the violation before and how recently.
  • Explains which factors raised the risk.
  • Suggests an intervention to follow up with the student.

Complaint Context Analyzer

Word2Vec + cosine similarity

Reads the description of a complaint and suggests which of the school's violations it most likely refers to.

  • Understands the meaning of words, not just exact matches.
  • Ranks the closest violations from strongest to weakest match.
  • Helps the Prefect of Discipline classify complaints consistently.
